# 11. Risks and Technical Debt

## 11.1 Risks

<!-- arc42-generated -->

| #   | Risk                                                                                                                           | Priority | Impact                                                         | Probability | Mitigation                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| --- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | -------- | -------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1   | Static SCEP challenge is a shared secret; anyone holding it can obtain a certificate for any name.                             | High     | Unauthorised client certificates                               | Medium      | Use `scep.challenge: dynamic` (single use, expiring, optionally bound); restrict SSM read access to the parameter.                                                                                 |
| 2   | `app-encrypted` only hides keys from console browsing; whoever can read both parameters can decrypt.                           | Medium   | CA key disclosure                                              | Low         | `customer-managed-key` or `kms` backend for high-assurance CAs.                                                                                                                                    |
| 3   | The encryption secret cannot be rotated; loss makes stored keys unrecoverable.                                                 | Medium   | Loss of CA keys                                                | Low         | `retain` removal policy; add new CAs to replace keys; back up deliberately.                                                                                                                        |
| 4   | Origin secret mismatch after rotation refuses all traffic (403) until the distributions are updated.                           | Medium   | Outage during rotation                                         | Medium      | Rotate in a quiet window; never delete the parameter by hand. Warm containers keep the secret they first read until recycled ([ADR 0010](../adr/0010-origin-secret-not-origin-access-control.md)). |
| 5   | The public Function URL is reachable without CloudFront; protection rests on the origin secret check alone.                    | Medium   | Bypass of mTLS and spoofed viewer-cert headers if secret leaks | Low         | Secret in SSM only; checked before any header is believed; rotate by version.                                                                                                                      |
| 6   | CloudFront trust store does not check revocation.                                                                              | Medium   | A revoked certificate passes the TLS layer                     | Medium      | Lambda checks status, expiry and owner for every renewal; documented in operations.                                                                                                                |
| 7   | OCSP and CRL freshness: cached OCSP answers lag a revocation by up to `maxAgeSeconds`; indexes are eventually consistent.      | Low      | Brief window of "good" for a revoked certificate               | Medium      | `ocsp.maxAgeSeconds: 0` where it matters; revoke path adds the just-revoked entry to the CRL explicitly.                                                                                           |
| 8   | Issuing CA expiry: renewal is "add a CA", not re-sign in place.                                                                | Medium   | Chain expiry if forgotten                                      | Low         | Follow the renewal procedure well before the 10-year expiry; monitor.                                                                                                                              |
| 9   | Single region, no cross-region recovery.                                                                                       | Medium   | Regional outage stops issuance and revocation answers          | Low         | PITR on the table; CRL and OCSP are cacheable; out of scope by design.                                                                                                                             |
| 10  | Offline root: signing a new issuing CA needs the root key restored to its store first; loss of the offline key blocks new CAs. | Medium   | Cannot add or renew CAs                                        | Low         | Store the key in an HSM or safe; test the restore procedure.                                                                                                                                       |
| 11  | Library defects (`@peculiar/x509` CRL serial encoding and PEM label) are worked around locally.                                | Low      | Wrong CRL if the library changes behaviour                     | Low         | Conformance tests with OpenSSL (`crl -verify`).                                                                                                                                                    |

## 11.2 Technical Debt

<!-- arc42-generated -->

No `TODO`, `FIXME` or `HACK` markers exist in `src/`, `lib/`, `bin/`, `test/` or `scripts/`. The items below are the deliberate gaps recorded in the ADRs, plus gaps found while writing this document.

| #   | Debt Item                                                                                                                                           | Priority | Impact                                                 | Source                | Remediation Plan                                                             |
| --- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------- | ------------------------------------------------------ | --------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1   | Issuing CA certificates carry no CRL distribution point or AIA URLs, so nothing points at the root CRL.                                             | Medium   | Chain validators cannot discover root revocation       | ADR 0006              | Needs a stable custom domain so the URLs are knowable at issuance.           |
| 2   | Old operational keys are not deleted after rotation (about one parameter per CA per year).                                                          | Low      | Slow growth of parameters                              | ADR 0007              | Delete superseded keys after expiry in the daily run.                        |
| 3   | CA renewal is "add a CA", not re-signing in place.                                                                                                  | Low      | Operational effort; devices must move to a new label   | ADR 0006              | Optional in-place re-sign command.                                           |
| 4   | SigV4 (IAM) authentication is not in the model; it shares `Authorization` with Basic and cannot sit behind the `cloudfront` front door.             | Low      | Automation must use Basic credentials                  | ADR 0011              | Decide a separate route layout first.                                        |
| 5   | MDM challenge validators (Intune, Jamf, Workspace ONE) are not built; Intune SCEP and API Gateway mTLS are unverified against real tenants/domains. | Medium   | Compatibility claims are partly untested               | ADR 0009, ADR 0017    | Integration test when a tenant and domain are available.                     |
| 6   | The resolved configuration travels in a 3000-byte environment variable.                                                                             | Low      | A large configuration fails synth                      | `lib/aws-ca-stack.ts` | Read configuration from a parameter or object if it grows.                   |
| 7   | No CI workflow, alarms or dashboards are defined in the repository.                                                                                 | Medium   | Regressions and a stalled CRL rely on manual vigilance | Repository contents   | Add CI for `lint`, `format:check`, `test`, `cdk synth`; add a CRL-age alarm. |
